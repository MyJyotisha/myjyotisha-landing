Cancellation and Refunds Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

1. Subscriptions (Nakshatra Weekly, Graha Shanti, Jyotishi Pro)

Subscription plans renew automatically each billing period until cancelled. You may cancel your subscription at any time from within the app or through your app store account settings (Apple App Store or Google Play), or by managing your subscription directly through the payment processor used at checkout.

  • Cancelling stops future renewals. You will retain access to subscription features until the end of the current paid billing period.
  • We do not provide partial refunds for the unused portion of a current billing period, except where required by law or app store policy.

2. One-Time Purchases (Janam Patri, Kundli Milan)

Janam Patri and Kundli Milan are one-time digital purchases that unlock additional generations/comparisons on your account immediately upon payment. Because these unlock digital content instantly, they are generally non-refundable once the purchase has been used to generate a chart or report.

3. When Refunds May Be Considered

We may consider a refund, at our discretion, in cases such as:

  • A duplicate or accidental charge.
  • A verified technical failure that prevented you from receiving the purchased content.
  • A charge made without your authorisation, subject to investigation.

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of the charge with your account email and order details.

4. App Store Purchases

If you purchased a subscription or one-time item through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, refund requests for those purchases are handled directly by Apple or Google according to their respective refund policies, as those platforms process the payment on our behalf.

5. India-Specific Purchases (UPI)

For purchases made via UPI or other Indian payment methods at myjyotish.app, refunds, when approved, are processed back to the original payment method and may take 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.
