Shipping Policy

Digital Service — No Physical Shipping

myJyotish is a fully digital service operated by Jyotish Technologies LLC. We do not sell, ship, or deliver any physical products. All features — including Janam Patri (birth chart) generation, Nakshatra guidance, Kundli Milan compatibility reports, Mangal Dosha analysis, and Muhurta recommendations — are delivered digitally within the myJyotish mobile app immediately upon purchase or account access.

Printed Reports

Where the app offers a "print" feature for a Janam Patri or Kundli Milan report, this generates a digital document (PDF or device share-sheet output) for you to print yourself using your own printer, or to save and share electronically. We do not print or physically mail any documents to you.

Delivery Time

All digital content, charts, and reports are generated instantly within the app upon request, subject to normal network conditions. Subscription features are available immediately upon successful payment confirmation.
