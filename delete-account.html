Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 2026

What gets deleted

When you delete your myJyotish account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account — email address and login credentials
  • All Janam Patri profiles — names, birth details, chart data for all profiles on your account
  • Vedic Analysis readings — all saved AI readings and career guidance
  • Compatibility reports — all saved Kundli Milan comparisons
  • Push notification settings — your device token and notification preferences
  • Billing country preference — your saved pricing country

⚠ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your Janam Patri profiles and readings will be lost. We recommend printing or saving any charts you wish to keep before deleting your account.

Note: If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) before deleting your account. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel subscriptions.

How to delete your account

RECOMMENDED — INSTANT

Method 1 — Delete from within the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the myJyotish app:

  1. Open myJyotish on your iPhone or Android phone
  2. Tap the Settings tab (⚙ icon)
  3. Scroll down to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted

Your account and all data will be deleted immediately. You will be signed out automatically.

EMAIL REQUEST — 7 DAYS

Method 2 — Request deletion by email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data retention after deletion

After your account is deleted:

  • All personal data is deleted immediately from our active systems
  • Anonymised usage analytics (if any) may be retained for up to 90 days
  • Transaction records required for legal and tax compliance may be retained for up to 7 years as required by applicable law
  • No personally identifiable information is retained after deletion beyond what is legally required

For full details on how we handle your data, please review our Privacy Policy.
