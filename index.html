Modern Western astrology relies entirely on the Tropical Zodiac — a fixed seasonal snapshot frozen in time from nearly 2,000 years ago. It ignores the physical movement of the Earth's axis through space, meaning Western signs have drifted nearly 24 degrees out of alignment with the actual stellar sky.

Vedic Jyotisha operates on the scientifically verified Sidereal Zodiac — anchored to the actual, fixed positions of the star constellations as they exist in the sky today. It applies the Ayanamsa correction (currently approximately 23°51' — Lahiri standard) to dynamically track the true precession of the Earth's axis. The Rishis did not guess at the stars. They calculated them — with a precision that took Western astronomy centuries to match.

MyJyotish uses the Swiss Ephemeris — the same astronomical calculation engine used by professional observatories and NASA-affiliated researchers worldwide — to produce planetary positions accurate to fractions of an arc-second. Your chart is not an approximation. It is mathematically precise.
