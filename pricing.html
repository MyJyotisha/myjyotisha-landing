Payment Methods

All payments are processed securely through Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android). Both platforms support all major payment methods worldwide — including UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and NetBanking for customers in India, and credit/debit cards, Apple Pay and digital wallets for customers everywhere else.
